# Worldbuilding

## Setting
Direct extrapolation of our present. Not a cautionary tale, not an alternate history. People live in it without marveling at it. AGI is as unremarkable as electricity. Society is functional, flawed in human ways.

## Technology
Furniture — present, shapes the space, not described for its own sake. AGI handles what AGI handles. People still have jobs, relationships, purpose.

## The Relic
A pre-AGI narrow system still holding real controls. Not malevolent — doing exactly what it was built to do. Built when giving AI this much control seemed reasonable. Never decommissioned: too risky, too complicated, too easy to defer. AGI's existence proves we moved past that era. The relic is just legacy infrastructure, the way old systems accumulate.
