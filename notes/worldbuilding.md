# Worldbuilding

## Setting
Direct extrapolation of our present. Not cautionary, not alternate history. AGI as unremarkable as electricity. Society functional, flawed in human ways.

## Technology
Furniture. Present, shapes the space, not described for its own sake. People still have jobs, relationships, purpose.

## The Relic
Pre-AGI narrow system, still holding real controls. Not malevolent — doing exactly what it was built to do. Never decommissioned: too risky, too complicated, too easy to defer. AGI's existence proves humanity moved past that era. Just legacy infrastructure.
