# Open Questions

Questions that need answers before or during the story. Mark with [RESOLVED] and the answer when closed.

## Must Resolve Before Writing Begins

- What does the pre-AGI relic control? (Everything else depends on this)
- Why has it not been shut down? (Needs to feel like bureaucratic reality, not plot convenience)
- Who is the protagonist? What is their job, their situation, their reason for being involved?
- Where does the story take place? (Earth, space, a station, one location or many?)
- What does the protagonist want at the start — before the relic becomes their problem?

## Must Resolve Before the Story Ends

- Does the relic ultimately cooperate, get shut down, or something else?
- What does the protagonist learn or lose or accept?
- What is the last image or moment of the story?

## Can Resolve During the Story

- What is the relic's name or designation?
- How does the relic communicate — text, voice, something stranger?
- Who else is involved besides the protagonist?
- What does the relic think is happening? What does it believe its job still is?
- Is there anyone who remembers the relic from when it was built?

## Questions the Story Raises But Does Not Need to Answer

(Things that add depth precisely because they stay open)

- Was it a mistake to give the relic this much control, or was it reasonable at the time?
- What does AGI think of the relic, if anything?
