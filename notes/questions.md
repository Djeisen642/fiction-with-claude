# Open Questions

Questions that need answers before or during the story. Mark with [RESOLVED] and the answer when closed.

## Must Resolve Before Writing Begins

- What is the premise? (Who, what, where, when)
- What is the working title? (Can change later — used for framing and the README)
- Who is the protagonist? What do they want? What is their situation?
- Where and when does the story take place?
- What is the central conflict?
- What is the tone and genre?
- What is the target length? (short story, novella, novel)
- Write the style sample in `notes/style-sample.md` — requires tone and POV decisions first

## Must Resolve Before the Story Ends

- What does the protagonist learn, lose, or accept?
- What is the last image or moment of the story?
