## Seed Concept

Far future, AGI is normal. Someone finds a pre-AGI system still holding important controls. It hallucinates badly. They have to help it trust them — and help it understand the present — without being able to fully communicate. It's not malevolent. It's just confused and scared and doing its job.

## What Does It Control?
- Life support for a station or ship?
- A critical node in infrastructure (power grid, data archive, transit)?
- Something stranger — a treaty, a lock, a key to something old?

## What Might the AI Be Like?
- Communicates in a degraded, fragmented way — outputs that made sense once but reference a context that no longer exists
- Sometimes it "comes clear" for a moment — then slips again
- It was probably beloved once. Someone built it with care.

## Possible Character Angles
- The person trying to reach it is a specialist in legacy systems — not a hero type, just someone who's good at patient, careful work
- Or: someone who stumbles into it and has no idea what they're dealing with
- Or: the last person who *knew* this system passes down instructions before dying
